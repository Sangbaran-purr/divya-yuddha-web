The Law

The Law of the Gate

The game is built into this gate, entered by the Access token.

  • The door is free. The Access token is claimed once at launch, one per hand, bound to you alone — never sold, yours to burn. It admits you; it buys no advantage.
  • The cards are owned. Every wave card is an NFT. Trade them on the marketplace in DYCoin, or earn them at the table — three cards a season, and three alone.
  • Two coins, one wall. DYCoin is money: the marketplace, and staking when it opens. The play-token is reward alone, redeemed toward the earnable three through verified play. Neither ever becomes the other.
  • Rank is not for sale. XP measures the player, and sets the bots you face. Player-versus-player matchmaking and staking arrive in Phase B — named here because they are coming, not yet here.
