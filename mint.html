The Plain Words

◈

What Liquidity Means for You

“Liquidity” is the pool of money that lets you sell your DYC on the exchange whenever you choose. More liquidity means easier selling at fairer prices.

The Cash-Out Desk Is a Facility, Not a Guarantee

“The desk pays USDT for reward-column DYC at a fixed rate of 0.010 USDT per DYC, from a reserve that DY Animation funds at its discretion. If the reserve is empty, the desk simply declines until it is refunded — your DYC is never taken, and it remains fully usable on the platform. Do not treat the desk as a promise that rewards can always be converted to cash at any moment.”

No Promise of Profit

DYC's staking produces DYC rewards under a hard lifetime cap of 2× per position. The cash value of DYC is set by the market. Nothing on this page is a promise that the coin's price will rise, and no one speaking for this project will ever make you one.

Two Coins, One Wall

DYC is money: the marketplace, and staking when it opens. The play-token earned at the tables is reward alone. Neither ever becomes the other.
